I am a gravitational wave astrophysicist, and my research lies at the boundary between simulation and observation. I'm paticularly interested in how we can use machine learning to improve and accelerate theoretical models of the the gravitational signals from mergers of compact objects (black holes and neutron stars), and how we use those models to infer astrophysical information about the black holes and neutron stars that made them. I'm an active member of both the [LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA](https://igwn.docs.ligo.org/web/website/) and [Simulating Xtreme Spacetimes (SXS)](https://www.black-holes.org/) collaborations.

I'm currently a [Postdoctoral Research Scholar Associate](https://www.pma.caltech.edu/people/lucy-thomas) at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech), working in both the [Theoretical Astrophysics Including Relativity (TAPIR) group](http://www.tapir.caltech.edu/index.html) and the [LIGO Laboratory](https://www.ligo.caltech.edu/). I previously completed my PhD at the [Institute for Gravitational Wave Astronomy](https://www.sr.bham.ac.uk/gwgroup/index.php) at the University of Birmingham, graduating in 2023. 

When I'm not doing science, I like to cook (especially baking), I love to sing, paint and travel.

Please find my [`CV/resumé`](/lucymthomas/cv/) and [`list of publications`](/lucymthomas/publications/), and you can reach via [email](mailto:lucy@lucy-thomas.co.uk) if you have any questions.
